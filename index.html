From Concept to Finished Product

HOW WE OPERATE

Building the easy hierarchy of development, design, and production.

01
Concept & Planning

Plant layout, clamp plan, weld plan, cycle time, and initial concept feasibility study.

02
Engineering Design

3D solid and surface parts modeling, concept design, and tooling feasibility study.

03
Simulation & Virtual Validation

Robot selection, reachability study, weld study, sequence creation, and cycle time analysis.

04
2D Detailing

Manufacturing drawings creation, sensor/pneumatic layout, and comprehensive BOM.

05
Tooling & Manufacturing

Precision jigs & fixtures, assembly systems, defense and satellite hardware, and CNC milling.

06
Commissioning

Online/offline programming, line integration, rigorous QA, and on-site delivery.
