Precision Tooling & Components

Manufacturing Excellence

Melecs is one of the leading manufactures of jigs and fixture, mylars, shims, sand and die casting tools. Our story of success in this competitive world of work is our determination, commitment and dedication to provide quality products to our clients.

As recognized we are involved in manufacturing and supplying a commendable array of precision components. We are highly appreciated for high quality and excellent finish of the products offered.

Commendable Precision Range

OUR MANUFACTURING SERVICES

High precision, durability, and superior surface finish across all manufactured components.

JIGS AND FIXTURES

Custom designed and manufactured welding, assembly, and inspection fixtures engineered for exact repeatable tolerance.

MYLARS

High precision contoured resting and clamping mylars fabricated from premium tool steel, urethane, and composite materials.

SHIMS

Precision thickness adjustment shims and packs ensuring micro-accurate alignment in BIW welding and tooling setups.

SAND CASTING TOOLS

Robust pattern making and core box tooling designed for complex heavy engineering and automotive cast components.

CNC MILLING COMPONENTS

High-speed multi-axis CNC milled components with tight tolerances and exceptional surface finish for mission-critical assemblies.

GRAVITY DIE CASTING TOOLS

Permanent mold tooling engineered for aluminum and non-ferrous alloys delivering superior dimensional consistency.

ELECTRONIC AND HARDWARE COMPONENTS

Enclosures, mounting brackets, fasteners, and specialized hardware components tailored for electronic devices.

AUTOMOTIVE, AEROSPACE AND HYDRAULIC COMPONENTS

High-pressure hydraulic fittings, aircraft bracketry, and automotive structural elements meeting rigorous industry standards.
